# Tanaad v3 · live demo script

Read from your phone. Every figure in quotes comes from the site visit. Where the screen shows a number, read the screen, not this page.

**Set-up, before anyone arrives:** open the demo in Chrome on the laptop. If you have a tablet, open it there too and put it on the table. Press Ctrl+. once and click Reset so the data is clean. Sign out. Internet off is fine; say so if it happens.

---

## 1 · Sign in · 1 min

Screen: the sign-in page.

"Two ways in. Office staff and the CEOs use an email and password. On the shared tablet on the factory floor, a worker taps their name and types a four-digit PIN. Nobody types a password with dirty hands."

Click **Shared tablet PIN**. Tap **Amina Nakato**, Supervisor, Mats. Type **1234**.

## 2 · Choose where you work · 1 min

Screen: the portals page, four cards.

"The business is one group with three operations: the mats factory, Tanaad Packages for bags, and the recycling plant. Plus a head-office view for the two of you. Amina is a mats supervisor, so only her card opens. The others are locked. People see their own site and nothing else."

Open **Mats Factory**.

## 3 · Today's Actions · 2 min

Screen: Today's Actions.

"This is the whole menu for a supervisor. Five items on the left, and in the middle a checklist of today's shift: mark attendance, register a new casual, contracts to review, breakdowns to resolve. Green is done. Amber needs her.

No training needed. If you can read a to-do list, you can run this screen."

Point at one amber item.

## 4 · Attendance & Wages · 2 min

Click **Attendance & Wages**.

"Today nobody, including you, can say how many people are at the mats factory. Here is the number, by department: extrusion, weaving, finishing, loading. Present, half day, absent. Marked by the supervisor with one tap per face.

Underneath, casual wages are worked out from the attendance itself. Casuals who vanish after a week drop off the list automatically. Their wage history never disappears."

## 5 · Production Report · 2.5 min

Click **Production Report**.

"Every machine, every shift. Who ran it, what it wove, how many. A fast loom makes about seventy-six small mats a shift; a slow one makes fifteen of the big 2.7 metre mats. One daily total hides that. This does not.

When a loom is well below its normal, the system asks why: power, mechanical, no string. And at the right, kilos in against kilos out: raw material, finished goods, measured waste. Material cannot leave through the back door without showing up here as a gap."

## 6 · Cash & Expenses · 2 min

Click **Cash & Expenses**.

"This is the cash-and-sales page the bags factory already keeps by hand. Opening balance, money in, money out, expected closing.

The one change: every line out must carry a category. Wages, materials, spares, utilities, transport, food, other. The page from the eighteenth of August that mixed a wage, compressor oil and lunch into one list of three and a half million shillings cannot be written any more. At close, the cashier counts the notes, the screen adds them, and any difference is visible before anyone goes home."

## 7 · Dispatch & Gate · 4 min · the centrepiece

Click **Dispatch & Gate**.

"The Rwanda shipment arrived two hundred mats short. One person counted, alone, and nothing was weighed. Here is how that becomes impossible."

Open the Kigali order.

"One hundred bundles of Rihlaat, fifty of Domona. Bundles are fixed counts with a known weight, so the system already knows what this lorry must weigh. Loading means scanning each bundle's sticker onto the lorry. Scan one twice and it refuses."

Scan the full load. Weigh.

"Scale reads, system compares. Inside tolerance: gate pass, delivery note, the driver signs."

Load the short lorry. Weigh.

"Ten bundles missing. The gate locks. The screen says exactly why, and both of you get a message on your phones within seconds. Only a site manager can override, with a PIN and a written reason, and that override stays on the record for good."

## 8 · The owners · 2.5 min

Sign out. Sign in as **Hassan Warsame** with the password. Open **Head office**.

"This is your screen, on a phone. CEO 3-Site Overview: mats, bags and recycling side by side. People on shift, output against target, cash in each safe, lorries out, decisions waiting. Nobody typed any of this. It is the sum of what the floor recorded today."

Click **Decisions & Approvals**.

"Export credit, gate holds, cash differences. You approve or hold from here, one tap."

Click **Morning Brief & Audit**.

"Every morning this arrives on WhatsApp before your first call. And below it, the record: every edit, every reopened day, every override, with a name, a time and a reason. Nothing is ever deleted. The monthly stock count can no longer go in the bin."

## 9 · Close · 1 min

"Three things. Record where the work happens. Check by arithmetic, not trust. See all three sites at once.

It works with no internet, it follows your own books, and every list in it, products, sizes, categories, machines, is yours to change without us.

What we need from you is answers to a short list: the bags process after extrusion, how the recycling plant pays collectors, and which registered company and TIN sits behind each site. With those we size phase one: people and cash at all three sites."

Stop. Take questions.

## If asked

- **Is this live data?** "Demo data built from what we saw at your sites. The screens and rules are real; the names are not."
- **Can we change sizes, products, categories?** "All of it, in Settings, without a developer."
- **Which devices?** "Tablets on the floor, phones for you, a laptop in the office. Same system."
- **If the internet goes?** "It keeps working on the device and sends when the line is back."
- **URA invoicing?** "Built for EFRIS. Connected once your credentials are in. Until then every invoice says plainly that it is pending."
- **How soon?** "Phase one is people and cash. We size it once your written answers are in."

## Never say

No revenue totals, scrap percentages or any number not on the screen or in the site report. Do not call the bags polypropylene. Do not say recycled pellet feeds the looms; it is sold as its own product.
