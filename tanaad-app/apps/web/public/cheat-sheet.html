If bundles are missing and weight is short by more than 2%, the gate status turns red, holding the lorry."

Tough Questions & Answers

Q: What if the factory loses power or internet?

A: It is an offline PWA. Supervisors continue logging attendance, machines, and cash on the tablet locally. When Wi-Fi returns, it syncs automatically to the cloud.

Q: Can supervisors fake numbers or delete records?

A: No. It uses an append-only ledger. Nothing is ever deleted. Any correction is appended as a new entry with an explanation and user timestamp.

Q: Can we roll this out one factory at a time?

A: Yes. We recommend starting with Mats Factory (Attendance + Looms), then Cash and Weighbridge, followed by Bags and Recycling.
